# Junbo Koh (고준보)

<p class="pub-links home-cv"><a href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/{{ site.data.profile.cv.google_doc }}/export?format=pdf">Download CV (PDF)</a></p>

<p>
Hello! I'm Junbo Koh (고준보), a public elementary school teacher in Seoul. I hold an M.A. in <a href="https://learning.snu.ac.kr/en/graduate-course/#:~:text=Educational%20Techonology%20Major">Educational Technology</a> with an integrated major in <a href="https://gsds.snu.ac.kr/integrated/#:~:text=%EC%9E%90%EB%A3%8C%EC%8B%A4-,%EC%9C%B5%ED%95%A9%EC%A0%84%EA%B3%B5%20%EC%9D%91%EC%9A%A9%20%EB%8D%B0%EC%9D%B4%ED%84%B0%EC%82%AC%EC%9D%B4%EC%96%B8%EC%8A%A4,-%EC%9D%B8%EC%82%AC%EB%A7%90">Applied Data Science</a> from <a href="https://en.snu.ac.kr/">Seoul National University</a>. I'm also affiliated with the university's <a href="https://ls.snu.ac.kr/eng/main/">Learning Sciences Research Institute</a> as a researcher.
</p>

<p>
My research focuses on <strong>human-centered AI in education</strong> with a theory-driven approach to addressing real-world educational challenges. I'm particularly interested in <strong>how AI systems can foster learner agency</strong> so that learners can chart their own learning paths and take ownership of their lives.
</p>

<p>
I'm currently exploring <strong>how LLMs can support language learners' agency through collaborative learning</strong>, using natural language processing (NLP) techniques. I'm also looking at <strong>how LLMs can foster learners' epistemic agency through productive failure</strong>, from a human-computer interaction (HCI) perspective.
</p>
